Standup Timer Setup

Install the bookmarklet to open the timer as a floating panel on top of Jira.

STEP 1
Deploy the timer
Upload index.html to a GitHub repo and enable Pages. Or host anywhere — you just need a URL.
STEP 2
Set your URL
Paste the URL where your timer is hosted:
STEP 3
Drag to your bookmarks bar
Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar:
⏱ Standup
If your bookmarks bar is hidden: Ctrl+Shift+B (Chrome) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac)
STEP 4
Use it!
Open Jira, click the bookmarklet. A floating panel appears with the timer. Drag it anywhere, minimize it, or close it. Click the bookmarklet again to toggle visibility.
FEATURES
Floating panel
✦ Draggable — grab the header bar to move it
✦ Minimize — click − to collapse, + to expand
✦ Close — click × to remove
✦ Toggle — click bookmarklet again to show/hide
✦ Stays on top of Jira — no tab switching
